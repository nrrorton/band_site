About Us

Nick Orton

I'm a software engineer in training with a stubborn desire to do something meaningful before the end. I'm rebuilding my life piece by piece, and life as a drummer plays a major part in this. So far, enjoying every bit of it and hoping to share a bit of this with those willing to lend me an ear or two.

Nick Sarwinski

Current musician and teacher of children who wants to be successful in every aspect of life. I'm constantly working to create the best sound possible for the people to hear. I am the younger 'Nick' who sings and plays guitar.
